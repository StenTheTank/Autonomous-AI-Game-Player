## 2. Game Structure & Key Strategic Principles

1. **Two Eras, Two Scoring Phases**:
   - **Canal Era (Rounds 1–10)**: Round 1 grants **1 action**; Rounds 2–10 grant **2 actions** each (19 actions).
   - **Canal -> Rail Transition**: At the end of Canal Round 10, all **flipped** tiles and **connected link dots (•)** score VP. Then **all Canal links** and **all Level-1 (L1) industry tiles** on the board are removed! **Level 2+ (L2+)** tiles remain on the board and can score **a second time** at the end of the Rail Era!
   - **Rail Era (Rounds 1–10)**: 2 actions per round (20 actions), culminating in final Rail Era Link + Flipped Building scoring.

2. **Flipping Industries Is Essential**:
   - Unflipped tiles on the board score **0 VP** and contribute **0 link dots (•)**!
   - **Cotton Mills, Manufactured Goods, and Pottery** flip only via **`sell`**, which requires:
     1. A connected path of links from the tile'"'"'s city to a **Merchant port** demanding that industry, AND
     2. Required **Beer barrels** (from *any* of your own unflipped Breweries anywhere on the board, a connected opponent unflipped Brewery, or the Merchant port'"'"'s bonus beer).
   - **Coal Mines and Iron Works** flip automatically when all of their resource cubes are consumed (selling directly into empty slots on the Coal/Iron market upon building can **auto-flip** them immediately for instant cash + income + VP!).
   - **Breweries** flip automatically once their beer barrels (1 barrel in Canal Era, 2 in Rail Era) are consumed by `sell` actions.

3. **Links Multiply Flipped Tiles (Single & Double Rail)**:
   - Each of your links scores the sum of link dots (`•`) on **all flipped tiles and active Merchant ports** at its endpoints.
   - In the **Canal Era**, canal links cost `£3` (max 1 per action).
   - In the **Rail Era**, you can build **1 rail link** (`£5` + 1 Coal) **OR 2 rail links in a single `network` action** (`£15` + 2 Coal, resolved sequentially so link 2 can extend link 1 + 1 Beer from any of your unflipped Breweries or a connected opponent Brewery; merchant beer cannot be used). Double-rail actions connecting dense hubs like **Birmingham**, **Coventry**, **Wolverhampton**, **Dudley**, **Walsall**, **Stoke**, or **Derby** routinely score **10–16+ VP** in one turn!

4. **Economy, Development (`×2`) & Loans**:
   - Flipping tiles raises your **Income track**, paying you pounds (`£`) at the end of every round.
   - **`develop`** removes 1–2 low-level tiles from your Player Mat (costing 1–2 Iron), either across two different industries **or twice on the same industry track (`×2`)**, unlocking **Level 2+ tiles** that survive the Canal transition and score twice (note: lightbulb Pottery L1/L3 cannot be developed away).
   - **`loan`** gives `+£30` immediately at `-3` income tiers. Early/mid-era loans unlock high-tempo builds when cash is low (`<= £6`), but avoid late-era loans (Rounds 9–10) or deep negative income spirals (`Still short` penalties cost `-1 VP` per missing `£1`!).
